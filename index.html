Dhankhar Organic Farms is our journey of building a farm brand from the ground up in Faizpur Ninana, Baghpat.

Today, our farm includes guava, lemon and turmeric. We focus on careful cultivation, protecting the value of fresh produce and building a transparent connection between our farm and our customers.

Our Story

Rooted in the land, growing for the future.

What started with farming is becoming a brand built around the soil, the people who work it and the produce it gives us.

Our farm currently grows guava, lemon and turmeric. We are continuing to develop the farm and expand our produce range as the journey grows.
